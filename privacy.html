HabitGrid Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

In short

HabitGrid collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking or analytics.

Where your data lives

Your habits, notes and entries are stored only on your device (in a local area shared between the app and its widgets). Nothing is sent to the developer or any server. You can export a backup (JSON/CSV) yourself.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; permission is optional.

Pro waiting list

The app collects no data. Pro is coming soon; joining the waiting list is optional and you send your email yourself to landezingcorp@gmail.com. We use the address only for that list, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. There are currently no in-app purchases.

Third parties

No third-party SDKs, ads or analytics are used.

Children

Since the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.
